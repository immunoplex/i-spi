**Sample wells** hold the actual test (patient) specimens — the measurements being
evaluated against the standard curve to back-calculate a concentration. See
[[glossary.standard_wells|Standard]], [[glossary.control_wells|Control]], and
[[glossary.blank_wells|Blank]] wells for the other well types a plate carries.
