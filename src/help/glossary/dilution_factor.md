The **dilution factor** is how many times a sample or standard was diluted before
being read — a value of 100 means the specimen was diluted 1:100. Back-calculated
concentrations are multiplied by this factor to recover the concentration in the
original, undiluted specimen. See
[[compute.import.dilution_source_precedence|where a well's dilution factor actually
comes from]] for how I-SPI resolves it during import.
