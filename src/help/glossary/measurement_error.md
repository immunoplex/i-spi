**Measurement error** (or assay noise) is the variability of a single response
reading at a given concentration — distinct from uncertainty in the fitted
calibration curve itself. The two sources add together (by the delta method) to
produce the full precision profile; see [[settings.precision_measurement_error|the
measurement-error toggle]] for how I-SPI lets you include or exclude it.
