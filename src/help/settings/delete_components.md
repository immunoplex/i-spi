id: settings.delete_components
title: Deleting study components
audience: user
category: procedural
---

Deletion here is **permanent and cannot be undone** — there is no trash or recovery
step. Choose a scope (the whole study, or a single experiment within it), then
**Preview** before committing: the preview runs a dry-run count of exactly what would
be deleted — raw plate data, the curve registry, fit results, dilution analysis,
antigen-family annotations, descriptive annotations, and any settings overrides for
that scope — without deleting anything yet. Only after reviewing the preview do you
confirm the actual delete.

Plate-level deletion is intentionally not offered: the smallest scope you can delete
is a whole experiment, not an individual plate within one.
