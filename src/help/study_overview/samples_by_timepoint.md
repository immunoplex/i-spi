The same plate-by-analyte sample-count grid as [[study_overview.samples_by_arm|Samples
by Arm]], broken down by timepoint instead of arm. Timepoint order is configured
under [[settings.annotations|Annotations]]; if no timepoint variable is defined
for the study, this view shows a notice instead of a plot rather than an empty
or misleading chart.

Use it to confirm samples are actually present at every expected timepoint
before relying on a longitudinal comparison.
