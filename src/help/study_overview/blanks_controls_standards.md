A plate-by-analyte grid showing how many [[glossary.blank_wells|blank]],
[[glossary.control_wells|control]], and [[glossary.standard_wells|standard]] wells
were read for each plate and analyte in the study — the non-sample specimen types
every curve depends on, independent of patient samples.

Use it to spot a plate or analyte missing one of these specimen types entirely, or
with noticeably fewer wells than the rest of the study — a gap here means a
curve, blank subtraction, or control check for that plate/analyte may be resting
on thinner support than the rest of the data.
