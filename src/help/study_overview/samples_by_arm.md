Shows how samples are distributed across study arms (treatment/control groups):
a plate-by-analyte grid of each arm's proportion of samples, plus a separate
distribution plot of participant counts per arm within each experiment. One arm
is marked as the **reference arm** — configured under
[[settings.annotations|Annotations]] — and always sorted first for comparison.

Use it to check that arms are balanced the way the study design intends; a
skewed or unexpectedly empty arm on a particular plate or analyte is worth
investigating before drawing conclusions that compare arms.
