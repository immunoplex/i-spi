This table lists every [[glossary.validation_errors_warnings|error or warning]]
I-SPI found in the uploaded workbook, highlighted red (error) or yellow (warning).
It runs automatically the moment the completed template is uploaded — there's
nothing to click here. [[import.wizard.commit|Commit]] stays disabled until this
list has zero errors; warnings don't block it, but are worth reading before you
proceed.
